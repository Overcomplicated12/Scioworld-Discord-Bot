# ScioCulture engagement plan

## Product story

ScioCulture is a social layer for ScioVirtual. It gives people a low-pressure way to answer, ask, joke, teach, vote, and contribute while they are already in Discord. The bot should make the community more interesting when people are present; it should not demand that they check in every day.

The central loop is:

1. **Notice:** a short question, poll, lore prompt, or course activity appears.
2. **Participate:** a member answers alone or joins friends.
3. **Understand:** the bot gives an explanation, context, or story consequence immediately.
4. **Contribute:** members can add lore, course questions, answers, or ideas.
5. **Return:** a new weekly prompt, story chapter, course set, or collectible goal gives the community a reason to come back.

## What makes it engaging

### Autonomy

Offer several valid ways to participate: trivia, races, polls, lore, trading, course questions, collecting, or simply reading. Do not make one activity mandatory for progression. Let members choose their visibility with privacy controls.

### Competence

Give immediate explanations after answers, keep questions short, and make progress visible through knowledge points, titles, sets, and streaks. A wrong answer should teach something and still give a small participation reward.

### Relatedness

Make the best rewards social and cultural: a funny lore entry, a shared title, a course trivia set, a community poll result, or a collectible that references a shared joke. Praise good explanations and teamwork, not only first place.

### Retrieval and spacing

Use short questions with feedback, revisit topics after a delay, and mix categories instead of repeating one subject in a single burst. This supports learning while keeping each interaction small. The bot should schedule optional weekly prompts and rotate content rather than turning the server into a checklist.

## Recurring rhythm

- **Whenever people are active:** `/trivia`, `/race`, `/camp-trivia ask`, and `/lore` create conversation on demand.
- **Daily, optional:** `/daily` offers a fact and small reward. Missing a day should not cause punishment or reset a meaningful achievement.
- **Weekly:** a community poll, a new question theme, or a shared challenge creates a common moment.
- **Monthly:** a lore chapter, collectible set, or camp-created trivia collection gives the community a larger arc.

## Content rules

- Every question has an explanation or source note.
- Every collectible has a story, not just a price.
- Community submissions are reviewed before becoming official content.
- Real students and instructors are never used as punchlines without consent.
- Course content stays within the permissions and privacy choices of the server.
- No gambling, wagers, paid random draws, cash-value rewards, or pressure to participate.

## How to tell whether it is working

Review these once per week during the pilot:

- unique participants and returning participants;
- percentage of questions answered and completed;
- number of members contributing lore or course questions;
- how often activities produce replies or conversations from other members;
- reward and collectible usage;
- opt-outs, reports, ignored prompts, and moderator interventions.

The goal is healthy repeat participation, not maximum message volume. If a feature increases spam, pressure, or confusion, reduce its frequency or remove it.

## Four-week pilot

### Week 1: discover

Use the announcement, `/start`, `/help`, and one friendly poll. Watch where new members get stuck.

### Week 2: participate

Add a themed trivia prompt and invite instructors to publish one course set. Track completion and answer quality.

### Week 3: contribute

Ask members for one lore entry, one question, or one collectible idea. Credit contributors in the story archive.

### Week 4: reflect

Publish what the community created, review the metrics, and ask a short poll: what should return, change, or stop?

## Research basis

- Retrieval practice improves learning compared with restudy, and distributed practice has a positive effect in authentic classrooms: [Educational Psychology Review meta-analysis](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10648-026-10225-5).
- Spaced retrieval has been associated with greater mastery, motivation, and attentional control in a classroom intervention: [PubMed study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42803776/).
- Gameful learning is more likely to support engagement when it supports autonomy, competence, and relatedness: [gamification literature review](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11159-024-10111-8).
- Discord recommends intentional onboarding so new members quickly understand where they fit and what to do: [Discord Community Onboarding FAQ](https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/11074987197975-Community-Onboarding-FAQ).
